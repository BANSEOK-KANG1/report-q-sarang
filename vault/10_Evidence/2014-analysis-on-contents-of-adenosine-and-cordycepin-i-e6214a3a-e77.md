## Abstract
Objective:To analyze the contents of adenosine and cordycepin in nine kinds of Cordyceps sinensis as well as three kinds of culture mediums. Methods:The adenosine and cordycepin were extracted with ultrasonic methods, and the con-tents of them were determined by RP-HPLC. Results:There was difference among the contents of adenosine and cordycepin in nine kinds of CML. The contents of adenosine varied from(1.054 ±0.259) mg·g-1to(2.931±0.356) mg·g-1and the contents of cordycepin varied from(0.435 ±0.016) mg·g-1to(4.981±0.212) mg·g-1. There was also difference among the three kinds of culture mediums; the contents of cordycep of culture medium No.3 was 1.046 mg·g-1, which was the highest; the content of adenosine of culture medium No.1 was 0.612 mg·g-1, which was the highest. Conclusion:There was difference among the contents of adenosine and cordycepin in nine kinds of CML as well as in three kinds of culture mediums.
