## Abstract
This study examined two samples of Ophiocordyceps sinensis from two main producing regions in Yushu stat and Qinghai Province( Haxiu and Guoqin). The content of moisture,ash,protein,aminoacids,inorganic elements,polysaccharide,adenosine,D-mannitol and cordycepin were analysed by the national standard method. Results showed that the amunt of each component was about the same in two samples: ash 4. 6% ~ 4. 7%,crude protein31. 57% ~ 33. 75%,total amino acid 23. 12% ~ 23. 73%,crude polysaccharide 10. 53% ~ 11. 19%,adenosine content 0. 18 ~ 0. 33 mg / g,D-mannitol 105. 34 ~ 131. 75 mg / g and no cordycepin was detected. The detection results will provide a good data foundation for Yushu Ophiocordyceps sinensis characteristic identification.
