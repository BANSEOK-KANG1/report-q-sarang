## Abstract
In order to explore the fungal secondary metabolites antifungal activity on P.nicotianae,in this study,fermentation broth of 75strains of C.sinensis and related fungi from all over the world were used in antifungal activity experiment on P.nicotianae by mycelium growth velocity.Bionectria ochroleuca,Paecilomyces tenuipes,Bionectria ochroleuca and Fusarium oxysporum and cordycepin were selected for in vitro cytotoxic test.The results indicated that 30samples(40.00%)had a strong inhibitory effect on P.nicotianae,and improved the effect with dosage increase.The cordycepin extracted from liquid fermentation broth of C.militaris had significant antifungal activity on P.nicotianae,and also improved the effect with dosage increase with the EC50value of 77.98 g/mL.
